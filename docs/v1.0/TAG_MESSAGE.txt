Argus v1.0.0 — first pilot

v1.0 is earned, not declared: a real boutique firm is using Argus in a
live pilot, within documented bounds (docs/v1.0/scope.md).

What 1.0 means:
- A cross-family verification spine (Anthropic synthesis x OpenAI gpt-4o
  judge + DeBERTa-v3 + lexical), enforced at boot — no silent heuristic.
- The measured guarantee: 0% false-positives-on-supported, 100%
  recall-on-insufficient on 61 human-labelled real claims (GREEN gate).
  Posture: "verified (conservative)" — never wrongly blesses, over-flags,
  human reviews flagged claims.
- Six-artifact deliverable suite, firm knowledge + layered modes, the
  full collaboration layer, observability, and the four enterprise
  pillars (isolation, retention, audit export, cost governance).

What 1.0 is NOT: SSO, app-level field encryption, external pen-test,
Companies House OCR, multi-instance/HA, notification digests/push,
element-level commenting, verifier-recall improvement — all named as
post-1.0 in docs/v1.0/scope.md.

The pilot is launched and running. Whether it's successful is the
retrospective's call, not this tag's.
