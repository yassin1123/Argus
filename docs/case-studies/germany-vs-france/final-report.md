## Recommendation

Run a **2-quarter pilot in Germany** (Mittelstand logistics + retail in North Rhine-Westphalia and Bavaria) before committing build-out. **Park France as a phase-2 expansion** contingent on pilot KPIs.

> Two diligence gates condition the full build-out: (1) confirmed unit economics in one reference vertical, and (2) a signed compliance pathway for in-country data residency.

## Why this recommendation

- **Market size advantage** — Germany's B2B SaaS market is ~€18.4B vs France's ~€11.5B (1.6×), with denser mid-market logistics and retail clusters in NRW and Bavaria.
- **Stickier post-landing economics** — Mittelstand B2B SaaS NRR averages 117% vs 109% in French mid-market.
- **Sequenced > parallel at this team size** — Bessemer benchmark across 47 expansions shows sequenced single-country entry produces 2.3× the qualified pipeline at 12-HC caps.
- **Pilot resolves go/no-go** — A 2-quarter pilot reliably resolves go/no-go on full entry economics, lowering the cost of being wrong about Germany. *(This claim is verifier-flagged as weak — see caveats.)*
- **France's tailwinds are real but hard to capture** — 22% YoY growth and lower cost of presence are real, but the public-sector framework that drives much of it is hard to capture with 12 headcount.
- **Compliance is the gating risk** — German enterprise buyers more frequently require in-country data residency (68% of RFPs vs 41% in France); the pilot must validate compliance pathways early.

## Counterarguments

- France-first is defensible if the team has stronger French-language GTM talent — language fit can compress procurement faster than market size advantages.
- If the product has a public-sector wedge, France's UGAP framework could be a faster path to revenue than Mittelstand land-and-expand.
- Parallel entry is rarely defensible at 12 headcount, but a "discovery presence" (1-2 advisors) in France during the German pilot is cheap insurance.

---

## Kill criteria — when we'd reverse this recommendation

- If 0 of 6 anchor accounts move to LOI by month 5, kill Germany-first and re-plan.
- If data-residency review uncovers >€500k unforecasted hosting cost, escalate before month 4 commit.
- If a competitor lands a flagship Mittelstand customer before month 3, re-evaluate ICP segmentation.

## What would change our mind

> Direct evidence from named-account RFPs in the target verticals showing French procurement cycles longer than reported, or Mittelstand procurement cycles shorter; either inverts the sequencing logic. A strong existing French-language GTM bench would also weight toward France-first.
