Our client (a £120m-revenue insurance-tech company) is missing a document-
intelligence capability their largest customers are now demanding. The exec
team is split on **build it in-house, buy a vendor, or partner/OEM** and needs a
recommendation for the board in two weeks.

The engagement library holds: the product team's capability spec, an internal
estimate of available engineering capacity over the next two quarters, and a
shortlist of three candidate vendors the corp-dev team assembled.

The decision needs:

1. **Time-to-capability.** For each option, the realistic time to a
   customer-ready capability given the client's stated eng capacity — not a
   vendor's happy-path timeline.

2. **Total cost of ownership.** A three-year TCO for build vs buy vs partner,
   including the things that get forgotten: ongoing model/maintenance cost for
   build, integration cost for buy, and margin share for partner.

3. **Strategic fit and lock-in.** Which option keeps the capability as a
   defensible part of the client's own product vs renting a commodity? Flag any
   vendor lock-in or data-ownership concerns.

4. **Recommendation.** A clear pick, the reversibility of each path (how hard
   is it to switch later), and the two or three kill-criteria the board should
   watch in the first six months.

State plainly where a number rests on a vendor claim that hasn't been validated
in a reference call.
